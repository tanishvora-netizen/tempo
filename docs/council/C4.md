# Council C4: submission red team

Question: red-team the app (localhost and screenshots), README, RESULTS, DEMO_SCRIPT, and SUBMISSION. What would make a Ramp engineer stop believing us, and what would break during a live demo?
Members: council-quant, council-controller, council-ramp-engineer, council-design, council-judge.

## Chair's position (written before reading any verdict)
Strongest points:
- Every number is generated from one precomputed build, and RESULTS, README, the demo script, and the submission are rendered by script.
- The certification is honest: Bonferroni on seed B, a separate seed C, and a true-model column.
- The AI layer is contained (locate-and-quote, verification, review queue, number and wording guards).
- Zero external calls, verified in a browser test.

Weakest points I expect to be attacked:
1. **The headline understates value.** The pre-registered K ladder certifies at 2.2%, and Tempo earns less than the uncertified smart spreadsheet. The sensitivity row answers it, but the demo must say it without sounding defensive.
2. **The evaluation shares a forecast family with the optimizer.** It is named as the main weakness, but a judge may still call it circular. The true-model column is the reply.
3. **The extraction eval flatters the parser,** because the generator and the parser share formats. The README says so; the demo should not lean on 100% precision.
4. **Synthetic data and the generator changes.** Both generator changes are disclosed, but "you tuned the data" is the obvious attack. The pre-registration in DECISIONS.md is the reply.
5. **Live-demo risk.** Shared state on the hosted demo, the 0.1-CPU host, and an `AS_OF` in the past at the Nov 19 final.

## Raw verdicts (round 1)

### council-judge: BLOCK (copy and script; demo readiness 6/10)
1. DEMO_SCRIPT close says Tempo "would fill in" EARLY_PAYMENT_DISCOUNT; it is a response-only value (SOURCES.md). Fix: "Ramp already records EARLY_PAYMENT_DISCOUNT; Tempo decides when to earn it."
2. Q&A 1 says the K = 10 plan is "as much as the spreadsheet, but with a guarantee": $49,282 < $50,243 and its true-model bound (5.25%) misses 5%. Fix: say Tempo trades about $9K for a certified 2.2%; consider a padded-buffer smart-spreadsheet row.
3. What-if screen: discounts + rewards do not add up to value; a card row nets a loss with no reason. Fix: a fees-and-surcharges row; card reason says it is used for float to stay above the buffer.
Missed by others: the showcased injection invoice is dated Nov 16, after the as-of date. Also: rehearsal changes saved state; SUBMISSION has placeholder URLs.

### council-ramp-engineer: BLOCK
1. `touched_at` is updated only by live solves and reset, so after 30 idle-looking minutes the next GET after an approval wipes it (reproduced). Fix: touch on every write; test.
2. Approve and the PATCH preview always read the precomputed plan; after a review edit the audited plan hash and payment dates are not the plan the user saw. Fix: use the live plan.
3. Behind Render's proxy the throttle sees one IP (no `--proxy-headers`), so all judges share 6 solves per minute. Fix: proxy headers in startCommand; prefer presets on the host.
Missed by others: the audit log lives on Render's ephemeral disk and restarts empty after every cold start; approve is not throttled.

### council-controller: BLOCK
1. The "ACH-safe fallback date" claim is false: the preview PATCHes the RTP date with method ACH, and for this week's RTP-dependent bills no safe ACH date exists. Fix: reword; move RTP-dependent bills out of PATCH into a "send by RTP in Ramp" bucket.
2. Late counts contradict: RESULTS shows LATE 0 everywhere while the overview says 5 arrive after the due date. Fix: "Late by choice" plus an "Arrives after due" count; state the late-fee treatment.
3. Approval is not a control (one typed name; auto threshold invites invoice splitting; "Verified" covers terms, not bank details). Fix: say Ramp's approval chain and vendor bank verification still gate payment; sum the same vendor in the same week for the threshold.
Missed by others: the audit entry stamps wall-clock time while the plan is frozen at Nov 10; record both.

### council-quant: FIX (math verified: Wilson bounds, Bonferroni z, ratios, proofs, row counts)
1. The true-model check swaps only per-customer timing; it reuses SIGMA_MACRO, P_NO_PAY, and the lognormal family. Fix: say so, and add a common-factor stress row (SIGMA_MACRO = 0.30).
2. Q&A 1 overclaims (as the judge says).
3. The 14-day Bluefin preset pays 5 bills late at only 2.47% risk; explain that in-sample K/N is 2%.
Missed by others: the seed-C point estimate (2.22%) exceeds the seed-B bound (2.16%), legitimate noise (about 2 sigma); "100% precision" is 55 of 55 (95% lower bound about 93.5%).

### council-design: FIX
1. What-if "decisions that flipped" reasons still quote the textbook 37.2%/yr while the run table shows the cash return.
2. An expected bill (issues Nov 12) is paid Nov 10 and flagged late. Fix: never pay before the issue date.
3. The approve dialog warns about 13 RTP-dependent bills with no list, fallback, or choice.
Smaller: sidebar jump on the injection screen; "$500.0K" / "$500K" / "$500,000"; payment run opens on late rows (sort by value).
Missed by others: the P10 band never comes near the buffer, so the managed risk is invisible; add a P1 to P99 band or show breaching paths.

## Consensus (round 1)
The math holds (quant re-derived every bound). What fails is truthfulness at the edges: two overclaims in the spoken script, a false RTP-fallback claim in README and Q&A, state bugs that would break the live Approve-to-preview step, an approval that recorded a different plan than the one on screen after a review edit, and paying an invoice before it exists.

## Strongest dissent
Controller: the payload preview claimed an ACH-safe fallback that does not exist for this week's RTP-dependent bills; previewing a PATCH that would make 13 bills late or lose discounts is worse than previewing nothing.
