# Council C2: the optimizer

Question: is the formulation correct, the risk bound honest, and the economics realistic?
Members: council-quant, council-controller, council-ramp-engineer.
Files: `tempo/optimize/` (milp.py, options.py, cash.py, certify.py, engine.py, baselines.py, evaluate.py, cash_value.py, explain.py, stats.py, run.py), `tempo/forecast/`, `tempo/assumptions.py`, `docs/RESULTS.md`, `data/demo/calibration.json`, `tests/unit/test_milp.py`, `tests/unit/test_planning.py`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

## Chair's position (written before reading any verdict)
The reduced formulation is exact and the tests (brute force, reduced = full, recount <= K) back it. The bound is honest in the narrow sense: seed B decides with a Bonferroni-corrected Wilson bound and seed C reports, and a true-model column shows 3.3% vs 2.4% under the fitted model. My concerns: (1) certification passes at the first ladder step with a large margin (B bound 2.3% vs 5%), so the ladder starting at K = 4 leaves value on the table; that is the directive's choice but worth naming. (2) The tension thresholds were missed and a fallback rule was pre-registered and used; the README must say so. (3) FINANCE_EARLY carries a made-up 1%/30-day fee and is chosen 16 times; it inflates value if the fee is unrealistic, so the README must label it an assumption. (4) Statement-date card float and the horizon-end clamp deserve a second look for end effects.

## Raw verdicts

### council-quant: FIX
Formulation checks out (quantile cut valid, M = q - R tightest, z only where rows exist with sum z >= n_z - K, Wilson correct, brute-force and full-vs-reduced tests pass).
1. RESULTS.md headline says "fresh scenarios", but seeds B and C come from the same fitted simulator, so certification covers sampling error only. Under the true model Tempo is 3.30% and pay_on_due 1.90%. Fix: label the headline "under the fitted forecast model" with the true-model row beside it.
2. options.py: card and FINANCE_EARLY have no capacity limit; FINANCE_EARLY beats BANK_DUE on every 2/10 bill and never touches the bank. Fix: card-limit and line-limit rows, or disclose.
3. run.py: "Bonferroni per-day" takes the 0.05/91 quantile of 1,000 scenarios with method="lower", which is the pointwise minimum; ablation deterministic_p50 uses the fan seed while the baseline uses seed A. Fix: rename or use ~20,000 scenarios; pick one seed.
Missed by others: fit.py fits on paid history only; invoices still open at AS_OF are right-censored, so mu is biased low (payments forecast too early). Fix: censored-lognormal likelihood with open receivables as survivors.

### council-controller: FIX
1. options.py:125 clamps an ACH send date to today but keeps arrival = due date (e.g. MEC-38628 "Paid on the due date Nov 10 by ACH"); these arrive about Nov 16. Fix: recompute arrival; flag late.
2. RTP scheduled on weekends and Veterans Day; Ramp rolls unavailable dates forward, which forfeits discounts, and the request enum has no RTP. Fix: send on previous_business_day for RTP and FINANCE; disclose RTP is expected, not instructed.
3. Early-pay sentences overstate: the rate quotes the headline d although the discount applies to the subtotal; rounding yields "about 9%/yr beats your 9%/yr"; the FINANCE sentence states the rate before its fee. Fix: dollars and the rate on cash actually paid, one decimal when close, net figure for financing.
Missed by others: approvals. PATCH works only on approved bills; Tempo schedules same-day sends without approval lead time and has no who/when/before-after log yet.

### council-ramp-engineer: FIX
Formulation sound; reproduced the committed plan hash; full case solves in 2.6 s.
1. Unlimited, unpriced financing inflates the headline: 16 bills ($818k) financed, worth $8,353 (17% of $48,750), on an assumed fee with no line limit and no Ramp API, invisible to "credit drawn". Fix: financing off for the headline, report with and without, add a limit.
2. Precompute hash is volatile (hashes every cache file while the background build writes them); the bundle was built with provider "none" (regex only). Fix: hash a frozen cache manifest; re-run precompute after the cache build; label the provider.
3. Ramp mapping incomplete: no payload builder yet; RTP-planned bills would arrive 2-4 business days late if Ramp routes ACH; `WIRE` vs Ramp's `DOMESTIC_WIRE`. Fix: build payloads; for RTP bills give an ACH-safe date or flag the RTP dependency.
Missed by others: card and financing are credit outside the risk model (December card charges $400k with no limit); the K ladder starting at 4 makes the effective target about 2.5%; greedy_p05 earns $4.1k more at 5.09%.

### Two-stage code review (spec, then python-reviewer), summarized
Spec: late-fee and surcharge sanity rules missing; node_limit not set; failed certification ships the last plan, not the best; weekly_run arrival not shifted; no tests for explain, cash_value, baselines, evaluate. Quality: build only pay_on_due where only it is needed; weekly_run window clause is a no-op; dead code in pipeline; provider_errors counts "no provider"; validate reviewer overrides; MAX_NET_DAYS constant; validate k < N and fit inputs; split long functions; ordinal "10th"; type hints on public functions.

## Consensus
The MILP and the reduction are correct and fast. Value is overstated by uncapped credit (financing, card) and by timing details (past-due ACH, weekend RTP); the risk claim is honest only inside the fitted model, and the fit itself is biased optimistic by censoring.

## Strongest dissent
Ramp engineer: financing is 17% of the headline on an assumed fee with no limit and no API. Turn it off by default.
